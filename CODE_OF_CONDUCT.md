# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want a welcoming, respectful community for people of every background and experience level.

## Expected behavior

- Be patient, constructive, and specific.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy, identity, experience, and differing viewpoints.
- Accept feedback gracefully and help others learn.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content and restrict participation when needed. Report conduct concerns privately using the repository's configured private security/contact channel. Until the owner publishes a separate private conduct contact, use GitHub private vulnerability reporting and label the report as a conduct concern. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
